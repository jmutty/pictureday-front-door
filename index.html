Common Questions

My school code stopped working.

School codes like NHS2627 are for early registration only and stop working three days before picture day. That is normal. After picture day, use Find my photos above: pick your school and we will look up your child's own code.

The flyer never made it home.

No problem. Pick your school or league above, choose "Find my child's code," and enter the details the way the school has them. If nothing matches, leave your email and a person will find it.

When will the photos be online?

School photos are usually ready about a week after picture day, sports photos within a few days. Log in with your code as soon as you have it and the gallery will email you the moment they are ready.

Is there a deadline to order?

Yes. Most galleries offer free shipping for the first few days and early-order pricing for a few weeks, with the dates shown at the top of your gallery. Galleries may be archived after the season.
